Response format: verbalised probability
Used by: MedGemma-4B, MedGemma-27B, Lingshu-7B, Lingshu-32B, DeepSeek-VL2-tiny, DeepSeek-VL2-small, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B

The question: based only on the available inputs listed above, should this lesion be considered suspicious for malignancy and referred for further biopsy?

Use label 1 when the available inputs support suspicion of malignancy and warrant further biopsy.
Use label 0 when the available inputs do not support suspicion of malignancy, including benign-appearing, low-suspicion, equivocal, nonspecific, or insufficient evidence for biopsy.
